1. Who we are

This policy applies to the GeoLink mobile game and related services (the "App"), operated by JanisD ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect information automatically when you download and use the App:

  • Device and technical data: IP address, device identifiers (such as a device ID or advertising ID), operating system and version.
  • Usage data: which parts of the App you use, when, and for how long.
  • Purchase data: if you buy something in the App, our subscription and purchase provider (RevenueCat) receives purchase status and an app user identifier. Payments are handled by Google Play, and we never receive your payment card details.

We don't ask for your name, and we only collect additional information if you contact us.

3. How we use it

  • To provide, operate and maintain the App.
  • To understand how the game is used and improve it (analytics).
  • To show advertising and measure how it performs.
  • To process purchases and restore them on your devices.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.
  • To respond to your requests and send important service or legal notices.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are: performing our agreement with you (providing the App), our legitimate interests (improving and securing the App), your consent (for personalized ads and non-essential tracking, where required), and legal obligation.

Where we share aggregated or anonymized data, it can't be used to identify you.

5. Ads and tracking

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and our other SDKs may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

You can manage ad personalization in your device settings: on Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads to opt out of personalization or reset your advertising ID. You'll still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

6. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the European Economic Area, where data protection laws may differ. When we transfer personal data, we rely on safeguards such as European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or your consent where required and permitted.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and request a portable copy of your data (GDPR);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We don't sell personal information. Some ad technology may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law, and you can opt out through the ad settings described in section 5 or by contacting us.

How to make a request. Email janisd.dev@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. When we delete your data, we remove it from our systems except where we must keep it for legal compliance or a legitimate business purpose. Some data may be needed for the App to function properly.

Uninstalling. Uninstalling the App stops further collection from your device. It doesn't delete data already sent to us or to third parties, so email us if you want that data deleted.

9. Children

The App isn't intended for children under 16 (or a higher age where local law requires), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them or market the App to them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, email janisd.dev@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

10. Security and breaches

We use physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect the information we process. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law, including what happened and what we're doing about it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with an updated date, and where the law requires it, we'll ask for your consent to material changes before they take effect. You can request earlier versions by emailing us.
